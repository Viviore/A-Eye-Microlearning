# A-Eye — PRD

## Problem
AI-generated fake images are getting easier to make and harder to spot. Young people see this content daily on TikTok, Facebook, Instagram, X, but most have no training to verify it. Traditional media literacy teaching (lectures, reading) doesn't engage this audience.

## Solution
A-Eye is a browser game where players act as digital investigators — inspect AI-generated images inside simulated social feeds, find inconsistencies, and decide if content is trustworthy.

## Target users
- Primary: youth / students (UNESCO Youth Hackathon audience)
- Secondary: schools, NGOs, youth orgs (post-hackathon scaling)

## Learning objectives (must-hit for judging)
- Recognize visual artifacts from generative AI
- Apply critical thinking before trusting or sharing images
- Tell apart observation, assumption, and evidence
- Understand how misinformation spreads across social media
- Build healthy skepticism without encouraging full distrust of legit info

## Constraints
- Timeline: hackathon submission window July 6 – Aug 16, 2026
- Must run without a custom backend/database for accessibility and low friction (static hosting only)
- Tech: Next.js (React) + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS

## Success criteria
- Pre/post assessment shows measurable improvement (improvement index)
- All 3 levels playable end-to-end
- Confidence calibration tracking works (self-rated confidence vs actual correctness)
- Feed simulation (posts/captions/reactions/comments) feels authentic enough to transfer learning to real platforms

## Out of scope (for hackathon version)
- Multilingual localization
- Educator dashboards

(these are "long-term vision" items in the proposal, not hackathon deliverables)
